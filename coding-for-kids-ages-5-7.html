Age-Specific Pedagogy

How Early Learners Code at STEMulus

We don't teach abstract syntax. We build stories, animated adventures, and simple games using visual iconography.

Sequential Reasoning

Kids learn that computers follow step-by-step instructions. They arrange visual action blocks in order to make characters walk, jump, talk, and dance.

Loops & Repetition

Instead of giving the same command ten times, students learn to wrap instructions in a loop - discovering the elegance of efficiency and patterns.

Creative Agency

Your child records their own voice, paints custom characters, and builds interactive animated storybooks to share with grandparents and friends.

Questions Parents Ask About Ages 5-7

Does my child need to know how to read fluently?

No. We utilize ScratchJr and custom visual icon tools where commands are represented by intuitive visual symbols (arrows, speech bubbles, sound icons, loops). As their confidence grows, early sight words are introduced naturally.

Can a 5 or 6-year-old stay focused for 60 minutes?

Yes, because sessions are 100% interactive and mentor-driven. Rather than a lecture, the lesson is structured as a series of 10-minute micro-adventures with storytelling, character drawing, and mini-breaks. Mentors adjust pace immediately if energy shifts.

Can parents sit next to the child during lessons?

We actively welcome it, especially during the free discovery session! You can see firsthand how your child interacts with the mentor and how proud they feel when their first project comes to life.
