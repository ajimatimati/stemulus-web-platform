Frequently Asked Questions

Does my child need any prior coding experience?

Not at all! This program is designed for complete beginners. We start from the very basics | what sprites are, how to drag and drop blocks, and gradually build up. Most of our5-9 year olds have never coded before joining.

What does my child need to participate?

For our online classes, your child will need a laptop or desktop computer (tablets won't work well for Scratch) and a stable internet connection.

What kind of games will my child create?

Students create a variety of projects including maze games, catching games, space shooters, interactive stories, and animations. By the end of the program, they'll create their own original game from scratch that they can share with family and friends!

What's the teacher-to-student ratio?

We maintain exclusive 1-on-1 sessions. Every student gets direct, focused time with their tutor — no distractions, no waiting.
