Common Traps for this Lesson
Colour Without Contrast

Light grey text on white looks stylish but is hard to read. Ask the student to squint at the screen.

Too Many Fonts and Colours

Limit the design to two fonts and a small palette so the layout, not the decoration, does the talking.

Indentation Mismatch

Python requires uniform 4-space indentation inside blocks, loops, and functions. Tab and space mixing throws IndentationError.

Rule of Two Questions (Zero Touch Typing)

Never take over the student's mouse or keyboard. Ask two progressive questions:

  1. "Look at the line right above the cursor: how does it differ from our example?"
  2. "If the computer reads left-to-right, what character did it encounter first?"
