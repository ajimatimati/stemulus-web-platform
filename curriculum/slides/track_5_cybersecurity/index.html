Track 5: Cyber Defenders

Defend the Digital Frontier: Cryptography, Packet Inspection & Ethical Defense

In an interconnected world, cybersecurity is the shield protecting human freedom and data privacy. Track 5 explores the principles of ethical defense: Caesar and Vigenère ciphers, public-key RSA cryptography, packet sniffing with Wireshark, password hashing, SQL injection defenses, and cyber hygiene.
