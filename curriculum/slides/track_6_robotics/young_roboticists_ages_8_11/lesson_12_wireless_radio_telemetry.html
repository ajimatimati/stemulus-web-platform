What to Look For:
• Radio transmitters encode digital 1s and 0s onto high-frequency carrier waves.
• Data packets contain headers, payload data, and checksum error verification.
• Packet-based wireless control forms the backbone of drones, rovers, and IoT swarms.
2.4GHz Radio Packets

Radio signals transmit data through invisible electromagnetic waves. Microcontrollers group characters into compact digital 'packets' sent over specific channel frequencies.

Channels and Addresses

To prevent interference in a classroom of 20 students, each controller-robot pair tunes into a unique radio group channel (e.g. Group 42).

Bidirectional Telemetry

Telemetry is not just one-way commands. The robot sends status data back to the pilot: 'Battery: 85%, Distance to Wall: 14cm, Status: OK'.

from microbit import * import radio # Tune to private secure channel group radio.config(group=42) radio.on() display.show("R") # Remote Mode # Handheld Steering Controller (Transmitter) while True: x_tilt = accelerometer.get_x() y_tilt = accelerometer.get_y() # Broadcast directional commands over 2.4GHz radio if y_tilt < -300: radio.send("FWD") display.show(Image.ARROW_N) elif y_tilt > 300: radio.send("REV") display.show(Image.ARROW_S) elif x_tilt < -300: radio.send("LFT") display.show(Image.ARROW_W) elif x_tilt > 300: radio.send("RGT") display.show(Image.ARROW_E) else: radio.send("STP") display.show(Image.HAPPY) sleep(50)

Common Traps for this Lesson
Only Capture Your Own Network

Packet capture is practised only on your own lab or approved sample files. Revisit the white-hat rules.

Loop That Never Ends

Check what changes each time round. If nothing changes, the loop has no way to stop.

Floating Input Pins

Unconnected digital inputs pick up static noise. Enable internal Pin.PULL_UP or Pin.PULL_DOWN resistors.

Rule of Two Questions (Zero Touch Typing)

Never take over the student's mouse or keyboard. Ask two progressive questions:

  1. "Look at the line right above the cursor: how does it differ from our example?"
  2. "If the computer reads left-to-right, what character did it encounter first?"
