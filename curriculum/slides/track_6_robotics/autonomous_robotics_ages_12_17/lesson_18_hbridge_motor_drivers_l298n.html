What to Look For:
• H-Bridges reverse current flow through a motor to switch spin direction.
• PWM on the Enable gate modulates average voltage and motor RPM.
• Separate logic and motor power rails prevent voltage brownouts on the CPU.
The H-Bridge Transistor Array

An H-Bridge arranges 4 electronic switches (MOSFETs) in an 'H' shape around the motor. Closing switches S1+S4 spins the motor forward; closing S2+S3 reverses polarity and spins it backward!

Inductive Flyback Protection

When a spinning motor stops suddenly, its magnetic coils collapse and generate a massive reverse voltage spike (Back-EMF). Flyback diodes safely channel this spike away from the microchip.

Dual Power Rails & Common GND

Heavy robots use two power supplies: a clean 5V/3.3V logic rail for the brain, and a high-current 12V battery for the motors—always tied together at a Common Ground (GND).

from machine import Pin, PWM import time # L298N H-Bridge Driver Pins for Left Motor in1 = Pin(25, Pin.OUT) in2 = Pin(26, Pin.OUT) ena_pwm = PWM(Pin(27), freq=1000) # 1kHz PWM speed control def set_motor(speed_pct): # speed_pct: -100 (full reverse) to +100 (full forward) duty = int(abs(speed_pct) * 10.23) # Map 0-100 to 0-1023 if speed_pct > 0: in1.value(1) in2.value(0) ena_pwm.duty(duty) print(f"Motor FORWARD at {speed_pct}% (PWM Duty: {duty})") elif speed_pct < 0: in1.value(0) in2.value(1) ena_pwm.duty(duty) print(f"Motor REVERSE at {abs(speed_pct)}% (PWM Duty: {duty})") else: # Active electromagnetic brake in1.value(1) in2.value(1) ena_pwm.duty(0) print("Motor BRAKE engaged") set_motor(80) set_motor(-60) set_motor(0)

Checkpoint Challenge

Demonstrate your understanding before starting your solo quest:

Why must the negative terminal of the 12V motor battery be connected to the GND pin of the 3.3V microcontroller?
Voltage is always measured relative to a reference point. Without a shared Common Ground (0V), the motor driver cannot tell whether a control wire from the ESP32 is HIGH or LOW.

Common Traps for this Lesson
Missing Common Ground (GND)

When using a separate battery pack for motors or servos, always connect battery GND to microcontroller GND so PWM signals have a 0V reference.

Powering Servos from 3.3V Logic Pin

Motors draw high stall current that causes CPU brownout resets. Power actuators from a dedicated supply rail.

Floating Input Pins

Unconnected digital inputs pick up static noise. Enable internal Pin.PULL_UP or Pin.PULL_DOWN resistors.

Rule of Two Questions (Zero Touch Typing)

Never take over the student's mouse or keyboard. Ask two progressive questions:

  1. "Look at the line right above the cursor: how does it differ from our example?"
  2. "If the computer reads left-to-right, what character did it encounter first?"
