What to Look For:
• ToF sensors measure photon round-trip travel time independent of target color or reflectance.
• Narrow laser fields of view prevent false echoes in tight corridors.
• Median and moving-average filters are essential before feeding sensor data into PID loops.
Photon Time-of-Flight (ToF)

Instead of slow sound waves, a ToF sensor (like the VL53L0X) fires invisible 940nm eye-safe laser pulses and times how many picoseconds light takes to bounce back!

Multi-Zone Sensor Array

By mounting 3 ToF laser sensors at -45 deg (Left), 0 deg (Center), and +45 deg (Right), a rover perceives a 3-zone spatial radar field simultaneously without moving parts.

Sliding Window Moving Average

All physical sensors occasionally produce a stray outlier glitch. A 5-sample Moving Average or Median Filter rejects spikes while preserving real obstacle trajectories.

Why is a Median Filter better than a simple Mean (Average) Filter for rejecting a single huge sensor glitch (like 9999mm)?
In the window [310, 9999, 290], the Mean is 3,533mm (causing a false high-speed crash!), whereas the sorted Median [290, 310, 9999] cleanly selects 310mm!

Common Traps for this Lesson
Forgetting to Divide Round-Trip Echo by 2

The pulse duration measures the sound or light traveling out to the wall AND back. Divide by 2 for one-way distance.

Missing Echo Timeout Guard

If no obstacle is in range, waiting forever for an echo freezes the robot. Always set a 30ms timeout.

Floating Input Pins

Unconnected digital inputs pick up static noise. Enable internal Pin.PULL_UP or Pin.PULL_DOWN resistors.

Rule of Two Questions (Zero Touch Typing)

Never take over the student's mouse or keyboard. Ask two progressive questions:

  1. "Look at the line right above the cursor: how does it differ from our example?"
  2. "If the computer reads left-to-right, what character did it encounter first?"
