Common Traps for this Lesson
Changing State Directly

Call the setter (setCount) instead of assigning to the state variable, or the screen will not update.

Lowercase Component Names

React components must start with a capital letter, or they are treated as plain HTML tags.

Skipping the User

Before moving pixels, ask who will use this and what they are trying to do.

Rule of Two Questions (Zero Touch Typing)

Never take over the student's mouse or keyboard. Ask two progressive questions:

  1. "Look at the line right above the cursor: how does it differ from our example?"
  2. "If the computer reads left-to-right, what character did it encounter first?"
