Comprehensive 10-Week Roadmap

10-Week Digital Art & Design Syllabus

Students progress from basic color theory and coordinate sketching to vector icon design, character sprite sheets, and a completed digital portfolio.

PHASE 01

Visual Foundations & Color Token Science

Weeks 1 to 2 · Digital canvas setup, color harmonies, and composition grids

Week 01
Digital Canvas & Coordinates

Resolution standards (DPI vs PPI), aspect ratios, coordinate grids, and brush physics.

Week 02
Color Theory & Token Harmony

HSL color spaces, complementary harmonies, mood lighting, and creating reusable digital palettes.

PHASE 02

Vector Illustration & Geometric Iconography

Weeks 3 to 5 · Bezier curves, geometric primitives, and character design

Week 03
Bezier Curves & Vector Paths

Anchor points, control handles, path combination, and precision vector logo creation.

Week 04
Character Archetypes & Silhouette

Visual proportions, dynamic silhouettes, facial expressions, and character concept sheets.

Week 05
Icon Systems & Visual Glyphs

Grid-aligned icon sets, stroke weight consistency, and UI symbol families.

PHASE 03

Pixel Art, Animation & Sprite Sheets

Weeks 6 to 8 · Raster grids, frame-by-frame animation, and game assets

Week 06
Pixel Grid Fundamentals

16x16 and 32x32 retro grid mechanics, dithering techniques, and restricted palettes.

Week 07
Frame-by-Frame Motion Timing

Keyframing, easing, anticipation, and animating an idle breath and run cycle.

Week 08
Sprite Sheet Production

Compiling texture atlases, hitbox margins, and exporting transparent animated GIFs and PNG sheets.

PHASE 04

Digital Brand Identity & Portfolio Showcase

Weeks 9 to 10 · Brand identity curation and live presentation

Week 09
Scene Composition & Lighting

Foreground, midground, background planes, ambient lighting, and environmental storytelling.

Week 10
Portfolio Exhibition & Defense

Curating original pieces into a responsive web portfolio and presenting live to parents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my child need prior drawing experience?

No prior drawing skill is required. Digital art at STEMulus focuses on computational geometry, shapes, color spaces, and vector coordinates. We meet every student at their skill level.

What devices and software are used?

Any laptop, desktop, or tablet works. We use industry-standard web tools including vector SVG editors, Piskel for pixel sprite sheets, and Canva for design systems. All tools run directly in modern browsers without costly licenses.

Can parents see the artwork created during sessions?

Yes. Every student curates their projects into a personal digital showcase portfolio. Parents receive monthly progress updates with direct links to high-resolution asset exports.
