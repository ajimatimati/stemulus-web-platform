About STEMulus Kids Tech

STEMulus KidsTech is Nigeria's leading premium online coding academy providing private, 1-on-1 live programming classes for children and teenagers aged 5 to 17. Founded in 2025, STEMulus is headquartered in Nigeria and primarily serves families across Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, as well as Nigerian diaspora families in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, and Australia. STEMulus also serves students worldwide including Ghana, South Africa, Kenya, UAE, and Singapore. Each session pairs a student with a dedicated expert mentor for personalized learning. STEMulus offers programs in Scratch, Python, web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), Arduino robotics, junior robotics, creative coding, digital art, AI and machine learning, and full-stack web development. The academy has taught students in over 20 countries. The first trial class is completely free with no credit card required. Sessions are conducted live via Zoom or Google Meet. Learning Journey

A clear progression from first block of code to deploying production-grade applications.

Creative Coding & Exploration

Game Creators
