About STEMulus Kids Tech

STEMulus is an online coding academy that provides private, 1-on-1 live programming classes for children and teenagers aged 5 to 17. Founded in 2025 and based in Lagos, Nigeria, STEMulus serves students worldwide. Each session pairs a student with a dedicated expert mentor for personalised learning at the student's own pace. STEMulus offers programs in Scratch, Python, web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), Arduino robotics, junior robotics, creative coding, digital art, AI and machine learning, and full-stack web development. The first trial class is completely free with no credit card or commitment required. Sessions are conducted live via Zoom or Google Meet. Contact: stemulusclubs@gmail.com | +2347052466716.
