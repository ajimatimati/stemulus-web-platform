How This Works

Submit this report at the end of each month. It routes directly to the STEMulus Admin team for review and approval. Once approved, an official, publication-grade academic report is dispatched to the parent. If the admin requests any adjustments, it will appear in your History tab with specific notes for you to redo and resubmit.
