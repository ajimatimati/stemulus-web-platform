FAQ

Do I need to buy any equipment?

No! The fee includes a complete Arduino robotics kit with all components. Your child keeps the kit at the end of the program to continue building at home.

Is prior coding experience needed?

Basic coding experience (like Scratch) is helpful but not required. We teach the programming concepts alongside the robotics. The hands-on nature makes learning code easier!

How do we learn robotics online?

We use a combination of virtual simulators (like Tinkercad) and physical kits delivered to your doorstep. Your personal tutor guides you step-by-step through circuit building and coding via high-definition video calls.
