Frequently Asked Questions for Ages 11-13

My child has never coded before. Is 11 or 12 too late to start?

Not at all! In fact, ages 11-13 have strong typing and logical maturity, allowing them to progress twice as quickly as younger children. Because every lesson is private 1-on-1, the mentor customizes the starting point exactly to your child.

What computer equipment is needed?

Any standard Mac, Windows laptop/desktop, or Chromebook with Chrome or Firefox browser and internet connection. We set up all coding tools (Replit, Python IDLE, VS Code) in the free first session.

How does this prepare my middle schooler for high school STEM?

Mastering Python and system logic before high school gives students an immense academic advantage in GCSE CS, AP Computer Science, math courses, and competitive hackathons.
