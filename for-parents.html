Parent FAQ

Questions parents always ask.

Can't find your answer here? Send us a message — we reply within a few hours.

What equipment does my child need?+
A stable internet connection, a laptop or desktop computer (Windows or Mac), and a webcam. All software we use is free to download. For Robotics programmes, we'll send you a specific kit list after enrolment — most items cost under $30.
What if my child doesn't enjoy their mentor?+
Mentor chemistry matters. If your child doesn't click with their assigned mentor — for any reason — we'll swap them for free, no questions asked. Getting the relationship right is more important to us than keeping a mentor booking.
How do I know my child is actually progressing?+
After every single session, you receive a detailed written report: what was covered, what was built, what was challenging, and what's planned for next time. You can also watch the full session recording from your parent dashboard whenever you like.
Can I enroll more than one child?+
Absolutely. Many of our families have two or more children enrolled across different programmes. Contact us after enrolling your first child and we'll apply a 10% sibling discount to the second subscription automatically.
What timezone do sessions run in?+
Sessions are scheduled in your child's local timezone. We have mentors available across multiple time zones and offer slots from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM local time, seven days a week.
Can I sit in and watch a session?+
Yes — always. You are welcome to join any session as a silent observer. We actively encourage this for younger children, and many parents do it regularly. Just log into the parent dashboard before the session starts.
What happens if we need to cancel a session?+
Sessions cancelled with at least 24 hours' notice are rescheduled at no charge. We understand that life happens — just message your mentor or contact us through the dashboard as soon as you know.
