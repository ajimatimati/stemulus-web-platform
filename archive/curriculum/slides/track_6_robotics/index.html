Track 6: Robotics, Physical Computing & Autonomous AI

From Circuits to Cognition: MicroPython, Sensor Fusion, PID Control, SLAM & Edge AI Rovers

Robots are where code leaps off the screen and moves the physical world. Track 6 takes learners from foundational digital electronics, PWM NeoPixels, servo linkages, and ultrasonic echolocation on the BBC micro:bit all the way to aerospace-grade autonomous systems on dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers: H-Bridge motor drivers, quadrature wheel odometry, closed-loop PID controllers, 6-DOF IMU sensor fusion, ROS 2 pub/sub architecture, Edge AI computer vision, 2D Occupancy Grid SLAM, A* path planning, and Swarm Robotics—100% playable in free browser simulators!
