What to Look For:
• Encoders convert mechanical rotation into digital pulse trains.
• Quadrature encoding uses two 90-degree phase-shifted signals to determine direction.
• Wheel odometry integrates tick counts over time to estimate robot position (x, y, heading).
Slotted Optical & Hall Encoders

A wheel encoder uses a spinning disc with slots (or magnetic poles) that rapidly breaks an infrared beam, generating a crisp square-wave tick for every fraction of a degree.

Quadrature Phase A & B

A single sensor counts ticks but cannot tell if the wheel is spinning forward or backward! Using two sensors offset by 90 degrees (Channel A and B) reveals both speed and direction.

Wheel Odometry Geometry

Distance = (Encoder Ticks / Ticks Per Revolution) * (Pi * Wheel Diameter). With a 65mm wheel and 200 ticks per revolution, each tick equals 1.02 millimeters of travel!

import math # Wheel Odometry & Velocity Calculator WHEEL_DIAMETER_MM = 65.0 TICKS_PER_REV = 200 def compute_odometry(left_ticks, right_ticks, dt_sec): circumference_mm = math.pi * WHEEL_DIAMETER_MM mm_per_tick = circumference_mm / TICKS_PER_REV left_dist_mm = left_ticks * mm_per_tick right_dist_mm = right_ticks * mm_per_tick center_dist_mm = (left_dist_mm + right_dist_mm) / 2.0 # Calculate instantaneous RPM and linear speed (mm/s) left_rpm = (left_ticks / TICKS_PER_REV) * (60.0 / dt_sec) linear_vel_mms = center_dist_mm / dt_sec print(f"Travel: {center_dist_mm:.1f} mm | Speed: {linear_vel_mms:.1f} mm/s | Left RPM: {left_rpm:.1f}") return center_dist_mm # Simulate 1.0 second sample where rover recorded 400 ticks per wheel compute_odometry(400, 400, 1.0)

Checkpoint Challenge

Why do robotics engineers use dual-channel (Quadrature Phase A & B) encoders instead of a single optical sensor?
By spacing two sensors 90 electrical degrees apart, Channel A leads Channel B during clockwise rotation, and Channel B leads Channel A during counter-clockwise rotation.

Common Traps for this Lesson
Missing Common Ground (GND)

When using a separate battery pack for motors or servos, always connect battery GND to microcontroller GND so PWM signals have a 0V reference.

Powering Servos from 3.3V Logic Pin

Motors draw high stall current that causes CPU brownout resets. Power actuators from a dedicated supply rail.

Floating Input Pins

Unconnected digital inputs pick up static noise. Enable internal Pin.PULL_UP or Pin.PULL_DOWN resistors.

Rule of Two Questions (Zero Touch Typing)

Never take over the student's mouse or keyboard. Ask two progressive questions:

  1. "Look at the line right above the cursor: how does it differ from our example?"
  2. "If the computer reads left-to-right, what character did it encounter first?"
