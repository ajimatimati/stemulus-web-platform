## Quick Instructions: How to Embed in Google Classroom

1. **Create an Assignment or Material** in Google Classroom under the appropriate Topic.
2. **Add Link:** Click **Add > Link** and paste the **Embed URL (GitHub Pages)** from the tables below.
3. **Tutor / Student Instructions Template:**
   ```markdown
   Classroom Mission Briefing:
   - Click the lesson link below to open your interactive 3D mission slides.
   - Press [Space] or [Right Arrow] to advance slides.
   - Press [S] to toggle the Speaker Notes drawer (Tutor Guidance & Child Metaphors).
   - Complete the interactive sandbox on Slide 2 and submit your Bronze/Silver/Gold quest on Slide 5!
   ```
