What is AI & Machine Learning?

Artificial Intelligence is the science of making computers think and learn like humans. Machine Learning is how we train AI using data instead of explicit programming.

This program demystifies AI | from how ChatGPT works to building your own image classifiers and predictive models. Python is the gateway to this exciting field.
