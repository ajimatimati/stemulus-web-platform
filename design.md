# STEMulus Bright Theme Design Guidelines

To maintain an elegant, accessible, and premium "Silicon Valley Editorial" visual identity, all pages, sections, containers, and components must adhere strictly to a pure light theme. Dark mode sections are completely forbidden.

---

## 1. Color Palette

* **Primary Backgrounds**:
  - **Paper (Warm White)**: `#faf9f7` (used for main page bodies, clean editorial sections)
  - **Alabaster (Cool White)**: `#f8f9fb` (used for alternating section blocks, light grid layouts)
  - **Cream (Warm Accent)**: `#FFF8EE` (used for callouts, CTA bands, highlight blocks)
  - **Sage Tint**: `#F2F6F2` (used for soft natural accents)
  - **Lavender Tint**: `#F5F2FA` (used for pathway highlights, timeline sections)

* **Typography & Text Color**:
  - **Deep Ink (Headings)**: `#1a2332` or `#0f172a` (slate-900 / var(--slate))
  - **Charcoal (Body Copy)**: `#4a5568` or `#475569` (slate-600)
  - **Muted/Helper Text**: `#64748b` (slate-500)

* **UI Accents**:
  - **Supernova Orange**: `#f4600c` (primary interactive elements, primary CTAs, underlines)
  - **Sage/Emerald Green**: `#22c55e` (success states, before/after comparison green highlights)
  - **Galaxy Purple**: `#6366f1` or `#4f46e5` (tutor badges, platform link outlines)

---

## 2. Component Guidelines

### A. CTA Bands (Call to Actions)
* **Background**: Must be warm cream (`#FFF8EE`) or paper-white (`#faf9f7`).
* **Heading**: `#1a2332` (slate-800) with a heavy font-weight (Nunito, 700).
* **Buttons**:
  - Primary button: Solid Supernova Orange (`#f4600c`) with white text.
  - Secondary button: Outline slate (`border-slate-300 text-slate-800 hover:bg-slate-50`).

### B. Footers (Editorial Newspaper style)
* **Background**: Cool Slate (`#f8fafc` or `#f1f5f9`).
* **Text**: Brand name and column headers must be Deep Ink (`#0f172a`). Links must be Slate-600 (`#475569`) transitioning to Slate-900 on hover.
* **Borders**: Soft thin dividers (`border-slate-200/80` or `1px solid rgba(10,10,10,0.06)`).

### C. Cards (Program Cards, Showcase Cards)
* **Background**: Pure white (`#ffffff`).
* **Borders**: Thin elegant borders (`1px solid rgba(244,96,12,0.12)` or `#e2e8f0`).
* **Shadow**: Soft ambient shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`).

## 3. Audit Findings — BYJU'S FutureSchool & WhiteHat Jr

Key operational and design mechanics audited from BYJU'S FutureSchool:

### A. Core UX & Conversion Components
* **Charm-Based Free Trial Hook:** A 45-minute live interactive session where a student builds a working app from scratch, culminating in a 15-minute live demonstration to the parents to establish immediate value.
* **1:1 Live Online Accent:** Direct, high-visibility visual highlights on interactive booking elements that emphasize live, personal, human accountability over pre-recorded videos.
* **Interactive Grades-Based Segmentation:** Segmented selectors for parents to filter the curriculum by age brackets (Grades 1-4, 5-8, 9-12), giving tailored project pathways for each group.

### B. Visual Design & Interactive Accents
* **Tactile 3D Buttons:** Satisfying, pressable elements utilizing offset translation and physical click active states to increase user engagement.
* **Milestone Highlight Loops ("WooHooMents"):** Short video highlights shared directly with parents via a dedicated app/portal showing the student explaining their custom code.
* **Silicon Valley Tech Copy:** Direct marketing references to high-concept engineering activities (e.g. self-driving car algorithms, SpaceX Falcon 9 simulation tracking, API-integrated AI chatbots) to inspire students.
