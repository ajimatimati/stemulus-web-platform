Frequently Asked Questions for UK Parents

Can STEMulus support my child with GCSE Computer Science?

Yes. Our mentors provide targeted 1-on-1 tutoring covering both Paper 1 (Computer Systems) and Paper 2 (Computational Thinking, Algorithms and Programming) across OCR, AQA, and Edexcel exam boards.

What days and times are available in the UK?

We offer slots Monday through Friday after school (from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM GMT) and throughout Saturday and Sunday mornings and afternoons. You can pick your ideal recurring weekly time slot.

How does payment work for UK families?

Tuition is processed securely online in British Pounds (£) with debit/credit cards or direct bank payment. There are no surprise fees or multi-term lock-in agreements.
