Frequently Asked Questions for Canadian Parents

Does STEMulus align with Ontario and BC curriculum guidelines?

Yes. Our curriculum explicitly reinforces the coding expectations in Ontario’s Grade 1-8 Science and Technology strand and British Columbia's ADST curriculum, while pushing ambitious students into independent software building.

How does scheduling work across Canadian time zones?

We accommodate students in Eastern, Central, Mountain, and Pacific time zones. When booking your discovery session, all times automatically display in your local Canadian time zone.

What happens in the free 45-minute discovery session?

Your child works 1-on-1 with a live mentor to build an interactive mini-project. The mentor evaluates their interests and logic skills, giving you a clear personalized roadmap. No credit card required.
