Frequently Asked Questions for Canadian Parents

What hardware or software does my child need in Canada?

A laptop or desktop computer (Mac, Windows, or Chromebook) with a reliable internet connection, working webcam, and microphone. All coding software used (Scratch, Python, VS Code, Replit) is free.

How does the free 45-minute discovery session work?

It is a real, live 1-on-1 session with a mentor. Your child will build their first hands-on mini-project during the call. The mentor assesses their learning style, and we share a personalized curriculum recommendation. No credit card is required.

Can STEMulus prepare my child for the Waterloo CCC competition?

Yes. We offer dedicated mentorship for the University of Waterloo Canadian Computing Competition (CCC Junior & Senior divisions), covering algorithmic efficiency, dynamic programming, and past contest problem sets.
