- All classes are ONLINE (virtual)
- Live instructor-led sessions
- Small group sizes (max 6 students per class)
- Duration: 1 hour for younger students (Ages 5-9) or 1.5 hours for older students (10+). Custom durations are available.
- Frequency: Twice a week.
- Platform: We use Jitsi Meet for online classes. A link is automatically generated (e.g. meet.jit.si/stemulus-trial-xxxx) when booking.
