Frequently Asked Questions for Nigerian Parents

What if my internet or power goes off during a class?

We understand the local Nigerian reality. If you experience an unexpected power outage or network disruption, simply notify your mentor and the missed session will be rescheduled at no extra charge.

How do I pay tuition in Nigeria?

Tuition can be paid seamlessly in Nigerian Naira (NGN) via direct bank transfer to our corporate account or online using your Nigerian debit card via Paystack/Flutterwave. Automated receipts are generated instantly.

How does the free 45-minute discovery session work?

It is a live 1-on-1 session with a mentor. Your child will write their first lines of code and build a playable mini-project live on call. The mentor assesses their learning style and recommends a personalized curriculum pathway. 100% free with no commitment.
