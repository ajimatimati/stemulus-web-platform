Frequently Asked Questions for High School Parents

Can STEMulus help my teen prepare for AP Computer Science exams?

Yes. We offer specialized 1-on-1 coaching for both AP Computer Science Principles and AP Computer Science A (Java), as well as UK GCSE and A-Level Computer Science. Our mentors review exam problem sets and reinforce core concepts.

How does this strengthen university and college applications?

College admissions officers love seeing tangible, self-directed passion projects. A live deployed web app, an active GitHub profile with commit history, and a completed capstone project provide concrete evidence of initiative, discipline, and technical acumen.

My teen has very heavy high school homework. Is the schedule flexible?

Completely flexible. Because sessions are private 1-on-1, you choose the recurring day and time that works best. If exam week arrives, sessions can be rescheduled with 24 hours notice at no extra cost.
