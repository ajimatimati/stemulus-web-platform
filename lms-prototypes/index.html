The Verdict & Recommended Strategy

The Winning Architecture: The "STEMulus Quest Hybrid"

No single archetype alone solves the full journey. Instead, the highest-performing architecture combines the best superpower of each:

🗺️

Course Navigation: Duolingo Path

Use the serpentine winding quest map as the macro curriculum route. Children instantly understand "where I am, what I beat, and what's next."

📖

Concept Deep-Dive: Frappe Clean Mode

When reading technical documentation, avoid noisy widgets. Use Frappe's ceramic distraction-free reader for pristine comprehension.

🧠

Knowledge Checks: Oppia Socratic AI

Replace punitive "WRONG!" banners with Coach Mulu's conversational guidance that catches specific cognitive traps and offers immediate repair.

🚀

Project Missions: Code.org Studio

Capstone missions embed the tri-pane studio where Junayd snaps blocks, switches to Python, and immediately watches his Mars Rover move.
